About Us

Who are we?

A free to attend social, networking and support group for the greater Ōtautahi transgender community.

We meet monthly at the Christchurch Workers Educational Association building, 59 Gloucester St from 7-10pm. A given meetup will typically have some kind of activity, icebreaker or other focal point, mixed with time to mingle, chat and eat snacks. All meetups are free, though donations towards venue hire are always welcome. We are working towards registered charity status, but this hasn't happened yet so for now, ice cream koha box it is.

Who can come?

We are for everyone who identifies in any way that falls under the transgender umbrella (yes non-binary people, that includes you!).
People who are questioning whether or not they are transgender are welcome as well, or can get in touch outside of meetups with any questions they may have.
In addition, cisgender people accompanying a transgender person, such as a parent, partner or supportive friend, are welcome.

What do I need to do/bring?

Yourself. We're pretty casual, no need to worry about dress code or anything. Contributing some of your favourite snacks to share or some money to cover venue hire is always welcome, but not required. You're welcome to bring dinner with you and eat it without sharing as well, just be sure to tidy up your rubbish.
Any exceptions to this will be said in the information section of the event, at the top of this page.

What is the venue like?

The CWEA is a hall, with a small kitchen and gender neutral bathrooms, as well as on occasion some additional smaller rooms that we hire for specific events. The building is fully wheelchair accessable. Parking at the rear of the building is limited to mobility parking only, but there is a parking building across the road. The CWEA is a short-ish walk from the bus interchange. No alcohol or smoking is permitted on site. Rubbish has to be taken away at the end, please be considerate of this.

When do you meet?

We meet every month, usually near the end of the month. The day of the week that the meetups are held on varies, so if you can't make it to one month's event, you may be able to make it to the next one. Events usually take place in the evening from 7pm to 10pm
